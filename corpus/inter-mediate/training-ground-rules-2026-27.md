# Training Ground Rules — 2026/27 edition

## 1.1 Session timetable

The weekly training schedule is published on the noticeboard and in the club app by 17:00 on the preceding Friday. It sets out the start time, the colour of the day, and whether each session is open or closed.

A standard training day runs as follows: players arrive by 09:30, gym activation starts at 10:00, the pitch session starts at 10:45 and finishes by 12:30. Afternoon sessions are held on Tuesdays and Thursdays during pre-season and when the head coach calls one.

## 1.2 Session types

The day after a match is a recovery day for players who played more than 60 minutes, and a full session for everyone else. Two days before a match is the main tactical session. The day before a match is a short session focused on set pieces.

Tactical sessions and the session on the day before a match are closed sessions. Closed sessions are attended only by players, coaching staff, medical staff and the head groundsperson.

## 1.3 Pre-season

During the first two weeks of pre-season, players train twice a day, with a morning and an afternoon session. The first three days include fitness testing, and every player completes every test, including players who were tested by their national team over the summer.

Pre-season start dates are confirmed before the end of the previous season. A player who returns late from the summer break is treated as absent without notice for each day missed.

## 2.1 Parking

Players park in Car Park A. Spaces are not reserved, with one exception: the space nearest the entrance belongs to the captain, and not to whoever owns the largest car.

The speed limit on the training ground site is 10 mph. It applies to everyone, including players who are late. Being late is fined; driving dangerously at the training ground is a disciplinary matter.

## 2.2 Access and security

Players enter Halfway Lane through the main gate using their access fob. The gate is open from 07:00 to 20:00. Outside those hours, nobody enters without the approval of the head of operations, given in advance.

A lost fob is reported to reception the same day. Fobs are never lent, and the gate camera records who uses each one.

## 2.3 Visitor parking

Visitors park in Car Park B, next to reception, and never in Car Park A. A player expecting visitors tells reception their car registrations in advance.

Visitors' cars are not left on site after the session ends. Reception locks Car Park B at 18:00.

## 3.1 Gym rules

Players sign in at the gym desk and follow the programme set by the strength and conditioning coach. Programmes are individual, and a player does not borrow a team-mate's programme because it looks shorter.

Equipment is wiped down after use. Boots worn outside are not worn in the gym. The strength and conditioning coach controls the music, and has asked that this sentence be included.

## 3.2 Gym hours and supervision

The gym is open from 07:30 to 19:00 on training days. Heavy lifts, meaning anything the programme marks with a star, are done only when a member of the strength and conditioning staff is present on the gym floor.

Players may use the gym on rest days with the agreement of the strength and conditioning coach, who will usually ask why.

## 4.1 Physio room

Treatment in the physio room is booked through the medical team, either at the desk or in the club app. Injured players have priority over all other bookings, and the head physio decides the order of treatment on the day.

Every knock, strain or pain is reported to the medical team on the day it happens, however minor it seems. A player who hides an injury to stay in the team is not being brave; they are making the medical team's job harder and the injury longer.

## 4.2 Recovery equipment

The ice baths, compression boots and hydrotherapy pool are next to the physio room and are used only with the agreement of the medical team. Ice baths are timed by a member of staff, not by the player.

Recovery equipment is not used as a place to make phone calls or hold meetings. The pool is not used for anything resembling a game.

## 4.3 Fitness testing

Every player completes a fitness test every six weeks during the season, on a day set by the strength and conditioning coach. The test covers speed, endurance and strength, and takes about an hour.

Results are shared with the player, the medical team and the coaching staff, and with nobody else. A player who misses a test without notice takes it on the next available day, at 07:30.

## 5.1 Visitors: general

All visitors to Halfway Lane sign in at reception, wear a visitor lanyard and are escorted at all times by the person who invited them. Visitors are requested through the team manager at least 48 hours in advance.

No visitors are admitted to closed sessions under any circumstances. Visitors do not enter the dressing rooms, the gym, the physio room or the video analysis suite.

## 5.2 Visitor limits

During training sessions at Halfway Lane, a player is allowed a maximum of 7 visitors at any one time. The limit includes children, and it applies whether or not the visitors arrived together.

Visitors watch from the viewing area beside Pitch 1. Visitors under 16 are accompanied by an adult at all times. A player whose visitors exceed the limit is asked to choose which ones go home.

## 5.3 Visitor conduct

Visitors do not photograph or film training drills. Visitors do not ask other players for autographs or photographs during a session; players may sign and pose after training, at their own choice.

No dogs are allowed on site, except assistance dogs. This includes small dogs, calm dogs and dogs that "love football".

## 5.4 Journalists at the training ground

Journalists come to Halfway Lane only for press conferences and for interviews the head of communications has arranged. They stay in the media room and its car park, and are escorted to and from both.

Journalists never watch training, from the viewing area or anywhere else. A player who sees someone they think is a journalist near the pitches tells reception.

## 6.1 Pitch closures for weather

The head groundsperson decides by 08:00 each morning whether each pitch is fit for use. Pitches are closed when they are frozen, waterlogged or being repaired, and the decision is not open to discussion.

When lightning is seen or thunder is heard, all outdoor sessions stop immediately and players go indoors. Sessions resume no sooner than 30 minutes after the last thunder or lightning. When outdoor pitches are closed, sessions move to the indoor dome.

## 6.2 Heat

When the forecast temperature at the training ground is above 30°C, pitch sessions start at 08:30 instead of the scheduled time. Water breaks are taken every 15 minutes, and the medical team may stop any session at any time.

Players are told about a heat change by 18:00 the evening before, in the club app.

## 6.3 Floodlit sessions

Pitch 3 is the only floodlit pitch at Halfway Lane. From November to February, afternoon sessions that would finish after dark are held on Pitch 3.

The floodlights are switched on and off by the head groundsperson only. They are not left on for players who want to stay out after the session.

## 7.1 Lockers and personal belongings

Every player has a locker in the training ground dressing room. Lockers are for clothes, boots and personal belongings, and are emptied at the end of each season.

The club is not responsible for valuables left in lockers. Watches, jewellery and cash are left at home or with reception.

## 7.2 Laundry

Training kit goes in the laundry bins at the end of every session, turned the right way out. Kit that is not in a bin is not washed.

The club washes club kit only. Personal clothing found in the laundry bins is placed in lost property, once, and then given to charity at the end of the month.

## 7.3 Boot room

Boots are cleaned in the boot room, not in the dressing room or the showers. Each player has a numbered space in the boot room, and boots are left there, not in cars.

The kit manager cleans boots on matchdays only. On training days, players clean their own.

## 7.4 Smoking and vaping

Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere on the training ground site, including in cars in the car parks.

The rule applies to players, staff, contractors and visitors. A visitor who breaks it is asked to leave, and the player who invited them is told why.
