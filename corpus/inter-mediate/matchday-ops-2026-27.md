# Matchday Operations — 2026/27 edition

## 1.1 Home reporting time

For home matches, players report to the home dressing room at the Middling Ground 2 h 15 min before kick-off. Reporting means changed into the club warm-up top, phone in its pouch and seated for the head coach's arrival.

The reporting time applies to every home fixture, whatever the kick-off time, including midweek matches and cup ties. Late reporting is fined under the Code of Conduct.

## 1.2 Arrival and parking

The player car park at the Middling Ground opens 4 hours before kick-off. Players enter through the players' entrance on the north side of the West Stand and show their access pass at the door, every time, including to stewards who have known them for years.

Players do not stop for photographs or autographs on the way in. There is time for both after the match, and the club would rather a player was late for a selfie than late for the dressing room.

## 1.3 Players not in the matchday squad

Players who are fit but not named in the matchday squad attend home matches unless the head coach excuses them. They arrive 90 minutes before kick-off, sit together in the directors' box, and are not in the dressing room before the match.

Injured players attend if the medical team agrees, and sit wherever the medical team puts them.

## 2.1 Pre-match timeline

All times are counted back from kick-off:

- 120 minutes: strapping and treatment slots open in the medical room
- 100 minutes: head coach's team talk in the dressing room
- 75 minutes: team sheet submitted to the match officials
- 60 minutes: warm-up kit on, boots checked by the kit manager
- 45 minutes: warm-up begins on the pitch
- 15 minutes: players back in the dressing room
- 7 minutes: the bell rings, players line up in the tunnel

The timeline is fixed. If kick-off is delayed, every step moves with it, and the team manager announces the new times.

## 2.2 Half-time

The half-time interval is 15 minutes. For the first 5 minutes the dressing room is quiet: players drink, see the physio if needed and say nothing about the first half.

The head coach speaks from the sixth minute. Players leave the dressing room when the bell rings, which is 2 minutes before the second half starts. Nobody is late back out. The officials notice, and so does the league.

## 3.1 Who is in the dressing room

From reporting time until the post-match address ends, only the following people may be in the home dressing room: players in the matchday squad, the coaching staff, the kit manager, the medical team and the team manager.

Directors, sponsors, agents and family members are not admitted, including on birthdays, including for "just a second". The team manager enforces this, politely, and then less politely.

## 3.2 Dressing-room rules

Pegs are arranged by squad number. The kit manager lays out each player's kit before reporting time, and players do not move or swap kit.

Music is chosen by the captain and played until 60 minutes before kick-off. After that, the dressing room is quiet unless the head coach says otherwise. The dressing room is left tidy after the match: boots in the boot room, kit in the laundry bins, and no tape on the floor.

## 4.1 Warm-up

The warm-up starts 45 minutes before kick-off and lasts 30 minutes. It is led by the fitness coach, and players follow it in full, including the parts they consider beneath them.

Goalkeepers warm up separately with the goalkeeping coach in the penalty area nearest the tunnel. The last five minutes of the warm-up are for shooting practice, and the shooting goal is the one in front of the home end.

## 4.2 Substitutes during the match

Substitutes warm up during the match on the touchline in the designated warm-up area, in groups of no more than three, as league rules require. They wear bibs at all times while warming up.

A substitute called to come on reports to the fourth official with shirt tucked in and shin pads on. The kit manager checks both before the player leaves the bench.

## 5.1 Team sheet

The head coach confirms the starting eleven and substitutes to the team manager by 90 minutes before kick-off. The team manager submits the team sheet to the match officials 75 minutes before kick-off.

After submission, the team sheet can be changed only if a named player is injured during the warm-up, and only as league rules allow. Players learn whether they are starting at the team talk, not from the internet.

## 5.2 Squad numbers

Squad numbers are allocated by the team manager in pre-season and do not change during the season. Number 1 is reserved for a goalkeeper. The name on each shirt must match the player's league registration exactly, including accents.

The number 12 is not issued. It was retired in 2009 in honour of the home crowd, after a pitch invasion that the club has chosen to remember fondly.

## 5.3 Captaincy on matchdays

The captain wears the armband and leads the team out. If the captain is not in the starting eleven, or leaves the pitch during the match, the vice-captain takes the armband. If neither is on the pitch, the armband goes to the player with the most first-team appearances for the club.

The captain tosses the coin before kick-off and speaks to the referee on the team's behalf. Other players do not approach the referee to discuss decisions; they ask the captain to do it, or they let it go.

## 6.1 Post-match duties

At the final whistle, players in the matchday squad walk to the home end and applaud the crowd, whatever the result. They then complete a 10-minute cool-down on the pitch, led by the fitness coach.

The head coach's post-match address follows in the dressing room. Players named for interviews then go to the media areas, as set out in the Media Policy.

## 6.2 Leaving the stadium

Players may leave the stadium once the head coach has dismissed the dressing room, and not earlier than 45 minutes after the final whistle. A player with treatment booked stays until the medical team releases them.

Players leave through the players' entrance. Players who drove to the stadium are asked not to drive away at speed, because the car park is shared with the stewards.

## 7.1 Access passes

Every player is issued a season access pass with a photograph. The pass is required at the players' entrance, the tunnel and the dressing-room corridor.

Passes are personal and are never lent. A lost pass is reported to stadium operations immediately, and a replacement is issued the same day. A player without a pass is let in after a phone call to the team manager, slowly.

## 7.2 Complimentary tickets

Every player in the first-team squad receives two complimentary tickets for each home match, in the West Stand. Requests go to the ticket office by noon two days before the match.

Tickets are collected at the ticket office window marked "Players" by the person named on the request. Complimentary tickets may not be sold, swapped or given away to be sold. Doing so is a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct.

## 7.3 Family room

Players' families may use the family room on the first floor of the West Stand before and after home matches. Each player may bring two adults and their own children into the family room. Entry is with the family room wristbands issued by the ticket office with complimentary tickets.

Players may visit the family room only after the post-match address and before leaving the stadium. Players do not visit the family room before the match, whatever the age of the family member asking.

## 8.1 Matchday medical cover

The club doctor and the head physio sit on the bench for every home match. An ambulance and a stadium medical team are on site from the time the gates open, as league rules require.

A player with a suspected head injury is assessed by the club doctor at the side of the pitch. If the club doctor has any doubt, the player is substituted and does not return to the pitch that day, whatever the score and whatever the player says. The head coach does not question this decision.

## 8.2 Emergencies and evacuation

If the stadium alarm sounds, players and staff stop what they are doing and follow the instructions of the stewards. The assembly point for the home dressing room is Car Park C, behind the East Stand.

Players do not go back for phones, boots or anything else. The team manager counts the squad at the assembly point and reports the count to the safety officer.

## 8.3 Postponements

If a match is postponed after players have reported, players stay in the dressing room until the team manager releases them. The head coach decides whether the squad trains on the pitch, if it is safe, or goes home.

Unless told otherwise, players report to the training ground at the normal time the next morning. The rearranged date is announced by the league, not by the club, and not by the players.
