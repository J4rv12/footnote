# Code of Conduct — 2025/26 edition

## 1.1 Who this code applies to

This code applies to every registered first-team player of Inter Mediate FC from the first day of pre-season until the last day of the season, including players on loan to the club. Coaching and support staff follow the separate staff code, but the club expects them to model the standards set out here.

Nothing in this code replaces the regulations of the Premier-ish League. Where the two disagree, the league's regulations apply and the club complies without comment, at least in public.

Every player signs to confirm they have read this code at the start of each season. Not having read it is not a defence. Having read last season's edition is also not a defence.

## 1.2 Punctuality

Being on time means being changed, ready and in the room at the stated time. It does not mean arriving in the car park at the stated time, and it does not mean being "basically there".

Reporting times for home matches are set out in the Matchday Operations handbook, and for away matches in the Away Travel handbook. Training start times are published on the weekly schedule by 17:00 on the preceding Friday. A player who has not checked the schedule is late on the same terms as a player who checked it and ignored it.

Fines for lateness are listed in the fines schedule in section 5.1.

## 1.3 Notice of lateness or absence

A player who expects to be late must phone the team manager before the stated time. A phone call means a phone call: a text message, a voice note or a message passed on by a team-mate does not count.

Calling in advance does not cancel a fine. It is recorded in mitigation, and it is the difference between being late and being missing, which the club treats very differently.

A player who is unwell must phone the club doctor before 08:00 on the day. The club doctor, not the player, decides whether the player trains, attends for treatment or stays at home.

## 2.1 Phones in team areas

Phones must be on silent at all times in team areas: the dressing rooms, the team meeting room, the video analysis suite, the gym floor and the physio room.

On matchdays, phones are not used in the dressing room from the moment a player reports until the head coach has finished the post-match address. Phones are placed in the numbered pouches on each player's peg. The kit manager will not look after a phone, will not charge a phone and will not answer a phone.

Phones are not used in team meetings or video sessions under any circumstances, including to check the time. There is a clock.

## 2.2 Photographs and recordings

No player may take photographs, record video or record audio in the dressing rooms, the physio room, the team meeting room or the video analysis suite. This applies whether or not the recording is ever shared.

Content for the club's own channels is captured only by the club's media team, who will ask before filming. Rules on personal social media accounts are set out in the Media Policy, not here.

## 2.3 Club devices

Tablets issued for video analysis remain club property. They must not be lent to anyone, used for anything other than club work, or left in a car overnight.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the head of operations within 24 hours. The cost of a replacement is deducted from the player's wages if the loss is not reported within that time.

## 3.1 Training dress

Players train in club-issued training kit only. The colour of the day is shown on the weekly schedule, and a player in the wrong colour trains in a bib for the whole session.

Players may wear their own boots and their own shin pads. No visible jewellery is worn in any session, including taped-over earrings, which the club has examined closely and does not consider invisible.

## 3.2 Travel dress

There is no travel uniform at Inter Mediate. The club does not issue travel suits or travel tracksuits. Players are free to travel in any clothing of their choice, provided it is clean, carries no offensive slogans and is suitable for being photographed getting off a coach.

This applies to every journey to and from a match, home or away, by coach, train or plane. Questions of taste are not a disciplinary matter, although the dressing room may treat them as one.

## 3.3 Official club functions

For official club functions, the club suit is required. Official functions are the end-of-season awards evening, sponsor dinners, civic receptions and any event the chief executive designates in writing.

Travelling to or from a match is not an official club function. The club suit is not worn for travel unless a function follows immediately on arrival, in which case the team manager will say so.

## 4.1 Alcohol

No player may drink alcohol within 48 hours before a match, on any club transport, or in public while wearing club kit or the club suit.

Outside those limits, players are adults and are treated as adults. A player who is unfit to train because of alcohol is treated as absent without notice.

## 4.2 Curfew

The night before a home match, players must be at home by 23:00. On overnight away trips, players must be in their hotel rooms by 22:30, and the team manager or a member of staff checks every room.

Curfew also applies on any night the head coach announces at training, with at least 24 hours' notice. A player who needs an exception, for a family emergency or the birth of a child, asks the team manager, who will say yes.

## 4.3 Gambling

No player may bet on any football match, in any competition, anywhere in the world, either directly or through anyone else. This includes matches the player has no connection with, friendlies, and markets that seem harmless, such as the number of corners or the time of the first throw-in.

Players must not pass team news, injury news or any other information that is not public to anyone who might use it to bet. A breach of this section is not fined. It is referred straight to a disciplinary hearing under section 6.1, and the club will also report it to the Premier-ish League.

## 4.4 Conduct in public

Players are recognised wherever they go, and are treated as representing the club whenever they are recognised. Players are courteous to members of the public, including those who are not courteous back.

Players do not argue with spectators, at the stadium or anywhere else. A player who is abused or threatened walks away and tells the team manager, who will deal with it. Players do not drive away from the stadium car park in a way that a reasonable person would describe as a statement.

## 5.1 Fines schedule

Fines are set for the season and apply equally to every player, whatever their wages:

- Late to training: £75
- Late to a team meeting or video session: £60
- Late reporting on a matchday: £400
- Missing training without notice: £900
- Phone used in a team area: £50
- Wrong training kit: £40
- Lost club device not reported within 24 hours: £200
- Breach of curfew: £1,000
- Breach of the alcohol rules: one week's wages

Lateness is measured against the stated time, to the minute, by the clock in the room.

## 5.2 How fines are issued

Fines are issued by the team manager, on the captain's report or on the team manager's own observation. The player receives written notice within 48 hours, stating the offence, the time and the amount.

A player may query a fine with the team manager within 7 days of the notice. A query is a request to check the facts, not a negotiation. Fines confirmed after a query cannot be queried again.

## 5.3 Repeat offences

A second offence of the same type in the same season is fined at double the amount in the schedule. A third offence of the same type is not fined. It is referred to a disciplinary hearing under section 6.1, and the hearing may impose a larger penalty than any fine.

The count resets on the first day of each pre-season.

## 5.4 Collection and use of fines

All fines are deducted from the player's wages in the month after the fine is confirmed. Fines are never paid in cash and never paid to the captain, however persuasive the captain is.

Every fine is paid into the club's infrastructure development fund, which pays for improvements to the training ground. The new floodlights on Pitch 3 were paid for entirely by late arrivals. Fines are not donated to charity. Players who wish to give to charity are encouraged to do so separately, voluntarily and with their own money.

## 6.1 Disciplinary hearings

Serious or repeated breaches of this code are heard by a panel of the head coach, the chief executive and the captain. If the captain is the subject of the hearing, the vice-captain sits instead.

The player is told the allegation in writing at least 3 days before the hearing and may bring a representative from the players' union. The panel may issue a written warning, a fine of up to two weeks' wages, or exclusion from the matchday squad for up to three matches.

## 6.2 Appeals

A player may appeal a hearing's decision in writing to the chairman within 10 days. The appeal is heard by the chairman and two directors who did not sit on the original panel.

The appeal panel may confirm, reduce or cancel the penalty. It may not increase it. Its decision is final within the club.
